# ProfitGuard Platform — product structure

## Product 01 — RetailGuard
Retail stock management:
- AutoOrder
- ABC/XYZ analysis
- OOS / OOS risk
- Overstock / Dead Stock / Frozen capital
- SKU-level inventory actions

## Product 02 — DistributionGuard
Distribution operations + Trade Marketing inside one module:
- Orders
- Inter-regional transfers
- Supply gap
- Warehouse / branch availability
- Trade Marketing inside distribution
- Turnover, promo, sell-out, AKB
- Region → Client → Package → Action

## Product 03 — SalesControl
Execution control for sales teams:
- Action-list
- Client reactivation
- Daily/weekly plan
- Responsible / deadline / status
- RACI logic

## Why Trade Marketing is inside DistributionGuard
Trade Marketing is not a standalone top-level product. Promo, turnover, sell-out and AKB actions are executed through distribution structure: region, client, package and availability.
